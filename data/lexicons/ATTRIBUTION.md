# Lexicon attribution

## warriner_2013_vad.csv

Norms of valence, arousal, and dominance for 13,915 English lemmas, slimmed to
the `Word` / `V.Mean.Sum` / `A.Mean.Sum` / `D.Mean.Sum` columns of the
published dataset (1–9 rating-scale means; the write path normalizes to 0–1).

- **Source:** Warriner, A. B., Kuperman, V., & Brysbaert, M. (2013). *Norms of
  valence, arousal, and dominance for 13,915 English lemmas.* Behavior Research
  Methods, 45(4), 1191–1207. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-012-0314-x
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0), as
  recorded by the NoRaRe database entry for this dataset
  (https://norare.clld.org/contributions/Warriner-2013-AffectiveRatings).
  Modifications here: column subset only; no value changes.
- **Why not NRC-VAD:** the NRC VAD lexicon is licensed for non-commercial
  research only (commercial use requires a separate NRC license), which fails
  this repository's Apache-2.0 compatibility gate.
